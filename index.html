The Nakkasi introduction

THE ESSENCE OF NAKKASI

An ode to her. A celebration of art. A legacy beyond time.

Some things are worn. Some are treasured. And some become a part of who we are.

For years, NAKKASI has celebrated something far more beautiful than jewellery itself. It is an emotion rooted in the richness of our heritage, the beauty of artistry, and the timeless bond between a woman and her jewels.

Every jewel carries a story, every detail reflects an artist's imagination, and every creation holds a certain magnificence. In its artistry, you find the grace of femininity, the splendour of prosperity and the regal beauty of traditions passed through generations.

From the grandeur of traditional heirlooms to the bold character of contemporary statement pieces, NAKKASI embraces every expression of this art, honouring its roots while celebrating its evolution.

And somewhere in every creation, you may find a reflection of yourself — your individuality, your grace, and the beauty that makes you uniquely you.

Because jewellery is never merely about what you wear. It is about what you cherish, what you express, and the stories that become a part of you.

Page 2 · The story behind Nakkasi

A Vision Born at Eleven

A Story Beyond Imagination.

A Vision Born at Eleven. A Story Beyond Imagination.

Some remarkable ventures begin with years of experience. Others begin with an extraordinary imagination and the courage to dream differently.

NAKKASI began with the vision of an eleven-year-old boy, Naman, whose prodigious mind, entrepreneurial instinct and fascination with the artistry of jewellery inspired him to create something of his own.

What began as an admiration for the beauty of adornment blossomed into a vision of a distinctive jewellery house, where heritage meets modernity, craftsmanship meets creativity, and every creation tells a story.

With an appreciation for the exquisite, the understated and the regal, Naman envisioned NAKKASI as more than a jewellery brand — a world where adornment transcends beauty to become an expression of art, individuality and timeless elegance.

From that first spark of imagination emerged NAKKASI, an evolving venture driven by creativity, determination and an enduring vision.

A word from the visionary
“To me, NAKKASI is much more than a brand. It is a dream I envisioned at eleven and a creative expression I wanted to bring to life. I believe jewellery should be more than something beautiful to wear; it should reflect individuality, embody artistry and hold meaning for the person who adorns it. I envision NAKKASI as a name synonymous with exquisite craftsmanship, timeless elegance and creations to be cherished, not merely worn. This is only the beginning.”
NAMAN · FOUNDER & VISIONARY

Co-Founder, NAKKASI

A woman of remarkable conviction, quiet strength and an unwavering spirit.

A woman of remarkable conviction, quiet strength and an unwavering spirit, Mamta is an integral part of the story behind NAKKASI.

As Co-Founder, she brings together creative instinct, an appreciation for artistry and a determined belief in the possibilities of the brand. Her contribution to its creative vision and design sensibility is an important part of what makes NAKKASI distinctive.

A pillar of strength behind the dream, Mamta embodies the belief that meaningful creations are built not only through imagination, but through perseverance, passion and the courage to see a vision through.

Her presence is woven into the journey of NAKKASI, making its story as much about shared belief as it is about a dream brought to life.
